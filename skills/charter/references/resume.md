# Resume

Bind and restate a charter without changing its substantive content.

Summarize its objective, status, current scope, decisions, definition of done, non-goals, referenced tasks, material review verdicts, and unresolved items. For a closed charter, lead with Outcome and residuals. Signal stale or contradicted Baseline evidence when the repository makes that visible; do not silently repair it.

Do not infer implementation progress from task references or repository changes. Stop after restatement unless the operator also asks to continue related work.
